Emergency Resources

You do not have to navigate a crisis alone. Free, confidential, and immediate help is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Please Do Not Wait

While I am deeply committed to your healing journey and long-term recovery, My Comfy Couch Counseling does not provide 24/7 on-call crisis services.

If you are experiencing a medical emergency, a life-threatening mental health crisis, or are in immediate danger of harming yourself or others, please prioritize your safety and reach out to emergency services immediately.

The resources listed on this page are staffed by trained professionals who are ready to listen, support, and guide you through difficult moments day or night.

Suicide & Crisis Lifeline

Free, confidential support for people in distress, prevention, and crisis resources. Available 24/7 via call or text.

Call or Text 988

Crisis Text Line

Connect with a volunteer crisis counselor 24/7. It is completely free, confidential, and accessible from anywhere in the US.

Text "HOME" to 741741

Veterans Crisis Line

Connect with the Veterans Crisis Line to reach caring, qualified responders with the Department of Veterans Affairs.

Dial 988, then Press 1

The Trevor Project

Information and support to LGBTQ young people 24/7, all year round.

Call (866) 488-7386

Emergency Services

For immediate medical emergencies, dispatch, or immediate physical safety concerns.

Call 911
