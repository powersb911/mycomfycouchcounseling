Counseling Background

I define the role of a professional counselor as someone who helps the client become the most positive form of themself through facilitating awareness within the client. My therapeutic approach has a holistic framework. My natural interaction is grounded in a Person-centered/Humanistic approach. However, I often use a non-directive Cognitive Behavioral method during my individual and group sessions. I believe the client can begin to materialize new internal and external resources to become a healthier individual by setting achievable goals and effective strategies while utilizing the gifts and talents the client possesses.

My counseling services address concerns or issues including, but not limited to:

  • Alcohol and substance use
  • Addiction
  • Depression
  • Anxiety
  • Shame, self-esteem, and self-image
  • Grief and loss
  • Trauma
  • ADHD
  • Autism Spectrum Disorder

Session Fees and Length of Service

Appointments will ordinarily be 50 minutes in duration, once per week at a time we agree on, although some sessions may be more or less frequent as needed. The time scheduled for your appointment is assigned to you and you alone. If you need to cancel or reschedule a session, I ask that you provide me with 24 hours’ notice. In addition, you are responsible for coming to your session on time. If you are late, your session will end at the scheduled time. Please understand that if you are beyond 10 minutes late, you will be asked to reschedule.

Cancellation & No-Show Policy: Clients using private insurance or private pay will be charged a $50.00 fee for any "no-show" or appointment cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice. Please note that insurance companies do not typically reimburse for missed appointments; therefore, this fee is the sole responsibility of the client.

Use of Diagnosis

Some health insurance companies will reimburse clients for counseling services, and some will not. In addition, most will require a diagnosis of a mental-health or substance use condition and indicate that you must have an “illness” before they will agree to reimburse you. Some conditions for which people seek counseling do not qualify for reimbursement. If a qualifying diagnosis is appropriate in your case, I will inform you of the diagnosis before we submit the diagnosis to the health insurance company. Any diagnosis made will become part of your permanent insurance records.

Confidentiality

All of our communication, including diagnoses, becomes part of the clinical record, which is accessible to you upon request (unless doing so would be psychologically harmful). I will keep confidential anything that you say to me, with the following exceptions: a) you direct me to tell someone else, b) I determine that you are a danger to yourself or others, c) I am ordered by a court to disclose information, d) I suspect or am made aware of physical/sexual abuse of minors, persons with disabilities, or seniors, e) I am working collaboratively with other professionals where disclosure of personal information is necessary to provide optimal care, and/or f) you are a minor for whom confidentiality is limited to the extent exercised by your parent/legal guardian.

In instances where clients participate in group counseling, I cannot guarantee confidentiality from other group members. However, I will do everything I can to ensure all group members understand confidentiality policies and that breaching confidentiality results in automatic dismissal from the group.
