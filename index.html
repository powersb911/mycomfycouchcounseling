The Practice

Life is hard,
your therapy
shouldn’t be

At My Comfy Couch Counseling, the goal is simple: to provide a sanctuary where you can finally just be human. I believe therapy shouldn’t feel clinical or cold—it should feel like a deep breath.

My purpose is to offer a non-judgmental space where adolescents and adults can unpack the weight of trauma, addiction, and anxiety. We exist to help you bridge the gap between where you are and where you want to be, turning vulnerability into resilience.

Here, you aren’t a diagnosis or a case file; you are a person worthy of grace, healing, and a soft place to land.

About Amy

A Human First,
Therapist Second

Hello and welcome. I believe the foundation of healing is a safe place to land, and my practice is built on the truth that we heal through connection.

With extensive experience in agency and non-profit settings, I specialize in helping adolescents and adults navigate substance use, trauma, anxiety, and major life transitions. While I am a Licensed Clinical Addiction Specialist and Certified Clinical Supervisor, I prioritize being a human first.

At My Comfy Couch Counseling, I trade clinical stiffness for genuine empathy. I’m here to listen without judgment and guide you with radical acceptance, ensuring you never have to navigate your recovery alone.

Let’s reclaim your strength together.
